## What's new in 0.34.0: the Tool map

A new way to see your whole project at a glance, a safer default for Draw, and a more readable Hotwheel in the dark theme. Old projects open unchanged.

**Tool map (press Tab)**
- Press **Tab** anywhere to see every tool and every document (each drawing, each Writer document, each board) as a glass bubble, with lines for the links between them. Click a bubble to go straight there.
- Lines stay very faint; the lines of the bubble you point at (and where you are now) stand out. Lines bend around other bubbles where they can.
- Bubbles float a little and lean toward the cursor.
- **Changed shortcut:** in Pixel Art, switching a tileset between **Tiles and Map** is now **Shift+Tab** (it was Tab). You can change it in Settings.

**Draw**
- **Draw beyond the canvas** is now a choice in the side panel (under Background), **off by default**. It needs a lot more memory, so a pop-up asks before turning it on. Drawings that already grew keep their extra area; they just stop growing while it is off.

**Hotwheel**
- The glass bubbles of the Hotwheel are now easy to read in the dark theme, with a lighter fill and a clear rim. The light theme looks the same as before.
