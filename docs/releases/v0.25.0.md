## What's new in 0.25.0: the export window

**Card Designer**
- **One export window for everything.** Export… opens a big window: pick where your deck goes on the left, see exactly what the file will look like in the middle (flip through the cards, front and back, with the cut line, safe line and bleed drawn on), and set it up on the right. It remembers your choices for each deck, and **Again** under Export repeats your last export in one click. You can export all cards, only the open card, or the ones you tick.
- **Bleed added on export, without touching your card.** The window uses your card's own bleed as it is and lets you add more just for this export: you see the card's bleed, the added bleed and the total. Fill the extra strip by stretching the edge, mirroring the edge (nice for textures), scaling the art (text stays put) or one colour. Your design never changes, so last-minute edits are no problem.
- **Print shops.** MakePlayingCards and The Game Crafter get one PNG per card side at exactly the pixel size they ask for (for example 822 × 1122 or 825 × 1125 px for poker cards), one back.png when all backs are the same, and copies.txt with how many of each card to order. There are also print-shop PDFs with one card per page and 3 mm or 1/8 inch bleed (the cut size is marked inside the PDF, which print shops check), and an "Other print shop" option where you pick the bleed and file type.
- **Print at home.** Any paper size, a setting to move the backs if your printer prints them a little off, and sharper "lossless" PDFs. Double-sided printing now also lines up on landscape pages (for example business cards on A4).
- **Photo station (janky).** For quick prototypes when nothing else works: JPG photos (9 × 13 up to 20 × 30 cm) with the cards at their real size. Put the back next to the front and fold it, or put backs on extra photos. Print one photo, measure a card, and if the photo station printed it a bit too big or small, type what you measured and export again to make up for it.
- **Godot.** Card pictures without bleed at the width you choose, with round see-through corners, either as separate PNGs or one sprite sheet with a .tres file per card, plus deck.json with all card data and res:// paths. You can leave the text out so your game draws it itself.
- **Warnings before you print.** The window points out text that crosses the safe line, missing pictures, pictures that will look blurry on paper, and bleed with empty spots. Click a card name to look at it.
