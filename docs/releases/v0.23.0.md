## What's new in 0.23.0: the card and cardboard update

Still no generative AI: every card, rule and stroke is yours.

**New tool: Card Designer**
- Design the front and back of a card and make a whole deck from one design. A new deck starts as a ready-made poker card with a background, picture, frame and text to change.
- **Real layers:** Select, the selection box, Ctrl+A, the arrow keys, Delete and recolouring only touch the layer you picked, so moving the art never drags the frame along. **Lock** a layer and nothing on it can be selected or changed. **Alt+click** an item to jump to its layer.
- **Your own stats:** add fields like Summon cost, Attack, Defense and Health (Text, **Number** or Picture). Type `{Attack}` in a text box and every card shows its own value. Long rules text shrinks to fit its box.
- **Icons in text:** type `:sword:`, `:shield:`, `:heart:`, `:mana:`, `:fire:` and 27 more, or add your own pictures as icons. They show in the text colour and size.
- **Quick recolour:** colour slots like Background, Frame and Accent change every card at once. **Variants** give factions or rarities their own colours, and **Recolour layer** paints a whole layer in one click.
- **Deck table or Spreadsheet:** fill in one row per card with how many copies to print, or link a Spreadsheet (row 1 names the fields; formulas show their results).
- **Card sizes** from poker, bridge, tarot, mini, euro and square to business cards and tokens, or your own in mm, with bleed and safe-area guides.
- **Export:** print sheets as **PDF** (exact paper size) or PNG on A4, A3, A5, Letter or Legal, with bleed, crop marks and backs on every other page for double-sided printing. Also the **whole deck in one picture** (10 per row, the grid tabletop simulators read), every card as PNG plus `deck.json`, or just the **deck JSON** for game engines.

**17 free fonts, built in**
- Fantasy, medieval, comic, sci-fi, retro pixel, classic book fonts, and easy-to-read and dyslexia-friendly fonts now come with the app, in every font menu (Writer, Draw, Pixel Art, tool fonts). All are free to use; their licences are in the app folder and credited in the README.

**Tabletop template**
- New projects made with the Tabletop / pen and paper template now include the Card Designer.
