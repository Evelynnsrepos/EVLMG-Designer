## What's new in 0.24.5: text boxes keep their fields on new cards, choice list fields

**Card Designer**
- **New cards keep every {Field} text box.** When a text layer was taken off every card (the chain on the layer), a new card from + Card came out without those text boxes, so the link to your fields (Name, Type, Rules…) was gone. + Card now copies the open card's boxes, and each new card fills them with its own values.
- **The T button keeps the field.** Switching a text box's T off (each card its own text) used to empty the box on all other cards. Now the other cards keep the box's {Field} links (like {Name}) and only lose the other words. With T on, the box keeps all its text on every card, new ones too, so labels like HP stay.
- **Choice list fields.** Set a field's type to Choice list and type its choices once, like Starter, Tank, Bruiser. Every card then picks its value from a dropdown. Old decks and the other field types work as before.
- **Older decks are repaired on open.** Cards that lost their field boxes this way get them back (copied from the first card that has them), and emptied boxes show their {Field} text again. A layer you switched off on a card stays off.

**Also in this version**
- Everything from 0.24.3 and 0.24.4: Duplicate in the sidebar, and card exports with pictures (Tabletop Simulator, PNG, PDF, print sheets) work again.
