## What's new in 0.24.6: fill your cards from a spreadsheet

**Card Designer**
- **Fill from spreadsheet.** A new button under the card list fills the deck from a CSV file (save or download your sheet as CSV UTF-8 from Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets) or from a Spreadsheet in your project. Each row is a card and row 1 names the columns. You choose where each column goes: a field, a new field (text, number, choice list or picture), Copies or a colour variant.
- **Update or add.** If a row has the same name as one of your cards, it updates that card. Every other row becomes a new card, laid out like the card you have open. Empty cells keep what the card already has. Ctrl+Z undoes the whole fill.
- **Unique text boxes from a column.** A column can go to a text box where each card has its own text (T off). From then on, that box shows the column on every card. Cards that aren't in the sheet keep their old text.
- **Pictures by file name.** Put a picture's file name in a column (like wolf.png), then click Pick pictures… to bring the files in.
- **Clear messages.** Anything that can't be filled, like a word in a number field or a missing picture, is listed by row.
- **Deck spreadsheet (CSV) export.** Export the deck as one row per card, edit it in your spreadsheet program, and fill it back in.
- **Cards from a linked Spreadsheet** now also get the items of layers that aren't on every card, the same way new cards do.

**Spreadsheet**
- **Dragging works again on Linux.** Selecting several cells by dragging, the fill handle, moving charts and resizing columns and rows now follow the mouse on Linux. A column or row border can now be grabbed from either side.
