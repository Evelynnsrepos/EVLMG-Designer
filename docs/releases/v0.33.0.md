## What's new in 0.33.0: a better Writer and spelling help

The Writer got a big overhaul, and spell check now really helps people who spell by sound and mix English and German. Draw also stops stuttering after each stroke. Old documents open unchanged.

**A much richer Writer**
- **Insert menu**: press the + button, or type `/` at the start of a line, to add headings, lists, checklists, quotes, callouts, tables, dividers and more. Each row shows the Markdown you could type instead.
- **Markdown help panel** lists every shortcut with an example, so you can learn them as you go.
- **Outline** of your headings (the list button in the header). Click one to jump there.
- **Find and replace** with Ctrl+F or the magnifier. Replace all is one Ctrl+Z.
- **View**: text size, line spacing and page width, to read comfortably, for all Writer documents on this computer.
- **Tables** from the + menu, with buttons to add and delete rows and columns.
- Crossed out text, Remove formatting, reading time next to the word count, and **Copy as Markdown** under Export.

**Spelling help made for real writing**
- Mix English and German freely, even in one sentence: each part is checked in its own language, and words both languages borrow (Gameplay, Cooldown, Bosskampf) count as correct.
- Fixes are sorted by how alike they **sound** and by how common the word is, so the right word usually comes first. The words around a mistake help pick the fix.
- **Writing hints** (dotted blue line) catch right-spelled wrong words: their/there, then/than, das/dass, seid/seit, a missing comma before dass or weil. Each card says in one line how to tell them apart, and one click fixes it. "Stop hints like this" turns a kind off.
- It **learns** the fixes you pick, and "Fix this word everywhere" fixes every copy at once.
- **Fix my text** (the underlined A in the Writer toolbar) walks through every underlined spot: Enter takes the fix, the right arrow skips, Esc stops.
- German nouns typed small get a calm hint, with one click to capitalise all nouns in the paragraph. Very long sentences (40+ words) get a hint to split them.
- **Calm underlines** (Settings) only shows underlines in the paragraph you are writing in. The speaker button reads the sentence aloud.
- Optional, off by default: **Fix sure slips while I write** corrects well-known slips (teh) and your learned fixes as you type. Ctrl+Z takes one back.
- No AI needed for any of this. The optional AI helper now checks sentence by sentence and never translates your text.

**Draw**
- Draw no longer saves the whole document after every stroke; it saves at good moments instead.
- No more short stall at the end of a stroke on some Linux systems.
