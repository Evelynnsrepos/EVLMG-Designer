## What's new in 0.30.0: smoother, faster drawing

Drawing on big pictures feels quicker, and fast strokes stay round. Projects and the file format are unchanged, so everything opens as before.

**Draw: round curves**
- Big, fast circles no longer come out as jagged polygons. The brush now follows a smooth curve through the
  points your pen sends; slow strokes and QuickShape outlines look exactly as before.

**Draw: faster on big pictures**
- While you paint, only the area under the stroke is redrawn instead of the whole picture. On a 4000x4000
  picture with 10 layers, each pen move went from about 840 ms to about 300 ms in our test, with the same result.

**Pixel Art: faster with many layers**
- The picture is redrawn at most once per screen frame, and the layers below the one you draw on are combined
  once per stroke. A 512x512 picture with 10 layers redraws in about 8 ms instead of 41 ms.
