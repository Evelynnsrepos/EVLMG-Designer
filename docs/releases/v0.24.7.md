## What's new in 0.24.7: decks ready for Tabletop Simulator

**Card Designer**
- **Tabletop Simulator (ready deck).** A new export saves your deck as a Tabletop Simulator saved object. The save dialog opens in Tabletop Simulator's Saved Objects folder (Windows: Documents/My Games/Tabletop Simulator/Saves/Saved Objects, Linux: ~/.local/share/Tabletop Simulator/Saves/Saved Objects). Click Save, then in Tabletop Simulator open Objects > Saved Objects and click the deck to spawn it. No importing, no typing grid sizes.
- **Card names and fields in the game.** Every card is named after its card, and its other fields show when you hover over it.
- **Big decks.** Decks with more than 69 cards become one deck made of several sheets.
- **Good to know.** The card pictures are saved next to the deck, and the deck points to them on your computer. Don't move them, and other players won't see the cards. For online games, use Tabletop Simulator (ZIP) and upload the sheets.
