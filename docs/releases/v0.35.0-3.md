## Unstable pre-release 0.35.0-3

> **This is an unstable test build, not a normal release.** It has new tools and big changes that are not fully tested yet. Back up your projects before you open them with it. The newest stable version is still **0.34.0**. Installed apps do not update to this build on their own, and the Flatpak from this page does not update itself.

Everything from 0.35.0-2, plus (all experimental):

**Writing and story**
- **Writer:** a tabbed toolbar (Home, Insert, Review, View, Markdown) with paragraph styles, small caps, colours, a table of contents, page breaks, comments in the margin, optional track changes, a Table tab, headers, footers and page numbers, and .html and .docx export.
- **Dialogue Editor:** a new Cards view. Type lines and choices right on the cards and drag to connect them. The old map is still there.
- **Storyboard:** type into the linked Writer text with Markdown.
- **Story Branch Writer** is back in the Add tool menu. A new scene switches back to Select.

**Planning**
- **Pitch Deck:** free slides you can place anything on, themes, a presenter view (P), transitions including Morph, and .pptx, .odp, PDF and PNG export.
- **Scope Planner:** versions with planned, added and cut features, a roadmap board, scope lock, shipping with carry-over, cut reasons and patch notes.
- **Playtests:** sections, 1 to 5 ratings, a Watch mode with a timer, and a bugs list. **Pacing Graph** can show the playtests (switch per journey).
- **Calendar:** a day planner for calendar games, with repeating entries, deadlines, moon cycle and a linked Timeline.
- **Achievements:** unlock rules, needs-first, groups, points budgets, quick setups for Steam, Xbox and PlayStation, CSV export.

**Numbers and systems**
- **Skill Tree:** boards (cells that open next to learned ones), any-of links, archetypes, your own node types and currencies.
- **Combat Simulator:** your own formulas, extra stats, buffs, turn mode, phases, waves and a time limit.
- **Economy Simulator:** formula amounts and a Markets tab. Combat, Economy and Balance have a cleaner card layout and German texts.

**Art**
- **Cosmos Creator:** real art in three styles (Painted, Pixel art, Flat), tinted with each body's colour, or your own set. Free CC0 art, credited in the README.
- **Draw:** layer buttons in one row, blend mode as a small icon button.

**Fixes**
- Draw: with "Draw beyond the canvas" off, the brush stays on the canvas, and turning it off can remove paint outside the canvas.
- Draw: "Keep my line" after QuickShape. Pick layer finds sign-brush letters. Tool keys like E should work again after you touch a slider.
- Writer: the grammar card opens only on an underlined word and closes when you type.
- Code Studio: long lines wrap instead of scrolling sideways (white area on Linux).
- Settings > General > Shaky hand help: a wobbly pen still clicks, and rows only start dragging after a clear move.

**Files:** all new settings are optional fields. Projects saved with this build still open in 0.34.0 and in the mobile app, which skip what they do not know.
