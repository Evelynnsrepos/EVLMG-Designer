## What's new in 0.24.10: better bleed

**Card Designer**
- **Stretch the card's edge over the whole bleed.** A new checkbox under Card size. When it's on, every print export fills the whole bleed with the card's outermost pixels, even where you placed art in the bleed.
- **Scaling with the bleed no longer moves your design.** The switch is now called "Scale the art with the bleed (exports only)". Your layout in the editor stays exactly as you made it and text boxes stay where you put them. Only exports scale the art, evenly so pictures and circles don't get squished, and turning the switch off brings everything back.
