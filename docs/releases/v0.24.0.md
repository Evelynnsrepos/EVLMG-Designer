## What's new in 0.24.0: the Card Designer, redesigned

**A new layout**
- **Left: your deck.** Colours, Fields and the card list are always at hand. The list shows each card's number, a small preview and its name, with a search box. **+ Card** adds a card and opens it.
- **Middle: the card.** The card menu above it now has a search bar, next to the ◀ ▶ arrows (or Page Up / Page Down) and Front / Back.
- **Right: Style, Card size and Export**, folded up above the layers.

**Pick the size first**
- A new deck starts by asking for the card size (poker, bridge, tarot, mini, euro, square, business card, token or your own in mm). Changing the size later scales the whole design along.

**Fields**
- Every field shows the shown card's value, ready to type. Open a field to rename it, change its type, delete it, or **apply its value to all cards** at once.

**Colours for the deck or for one card**
- Each colour slot has a **chain**: linked, it is the same on every card (✓ all). Unlink it and only the shown card gets its own colour (this card).
- Click a slot to pick its colour with **Draw's colour wheel**, recent colours and palettes.
- The card's **variant** is a dropdown right in Colours.

**Layers for the deck or for one card**
- Every layer shows a small preview and can be renamed with its pencil.
- The **chain** on a layer makes it **per card**: each card can move or change what is on that layer for itself, while everything else stays the same on every card.
- **Turn a layer off on one card** with the no-entry button, and make a whole layer **foil** with the sparkle.

**A tidier tool bar and the Hotwheel**
- The bar under the card keeps Select, Text, a picture field and a fixed image. Rectangle, ellipse and line sit in one **Shapes** menu (R, O and L still work), and panning is Space+drag.
- The layers' own X hides them, and **Show layers** brings them back.
- The **Hotwheel** now works in the Card Designer: previous / next card, Front / Back, + Card, Text, Rectangle, Foil and Lock layer.

**Draw: card canvases**
- Draw's new-drawing screen has the card sizes too, at print resolution with 3 mm bleed, and shows the cut line and the safe area as guide lines (switch them off in the side panel).
