## What's new in 0.17.0

Still no generative AI: every idea and every stroke is yours.

**Jump points: connect everything**
- Every tool has a jump points button in its header (next to Review). Add a jump point to any tool, document or entry: a drawing, a board, a level, a gameplay loop, writing principles, a character, an item… A searchable window shows everything there is.
- The same button lists what points back here, so you can always return. Characters, items, towns and enemies show the jump points that lead to them in their Jump to bar.
- On every board (Moodboard, Design Language, Brainstorm, Map, Story, Timeline, UI Flow) the jump tool (J) places a jump point right on the board: click where it goes, pick its target, and double-click the chip to jump.
- The user guide has a new Jump points page, and the tour and the crash course show it.

**Draw**
- Merge down, flattening a group and deleting layers are one undo step each: Ctrl+Z brings the layers and their pixels back, Ctrl+Y does it again. Merging no longer loses layers.
- Sliders, menus and buttons inside a layer or a brush no longer grab and drag the whole row, with a pen or a mouse.

**Boards**
- In a narrow (split) panel the bottom toolbar wraps onto a second row instead of scrolling out of sight.
- Any colour: the palette button after the colour swatches opens the colour wheel with your recent and saved colours, on the Moodboard, Design Language and Brainstorm Board.
