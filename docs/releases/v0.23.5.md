## What's new in 0.23.5: what goes on every card

**Card Designer**
- **Everything that is on every card shows in the accent colour**: layers and the items in them.
- **The layer chain works now** (and lights up when it is on). Click it to take a layer off every card: each card gets its own copy of what is on it, to change for itself.
- **Every item has its own chain**: keep it on the shown card only, or put it on every card. Putting an item on every card puts its layer on every card too.
- **Every text box has a T button**: lit, its text goes with it to every card that has the box; off, each card types its own text and the box starts empty on the other cards.
- **+ Add under every layer** puts a text box, a new text or number field with its box, a picture field, an image or a shape on that layer.
- **Fields follow the layers**: type {Power} in a text box and a Power field appears in Fields.
- An item you take off every card keeps its place in the layer instead of jumping to the top.
- **Linux:** btop and top show the app as EVLMG 0.23.5.

Decks made with 0.23.3 that had per-card layers are converted when you open them; nothing is lost.
