## What's new in 0.21.0

Still no generative AI: every idea and every stroke is yours.

**A new name: Eve.Lopment Games Designer**
- The app is now called **Eve.Lopment Games Designer**, or **EVLMG Designer** for short, with a new logo.
- Nothing to reinstall: it updates over your old version, and your projects stay where they are.
- The guide links to the new website and to Ko-fi, if you'd like to support the app. It stays free either way.

**Folders on the start screen**
- **New folder** next to the search box makes a section for your projects, for example Games, YouTube videos or Writing.
- Drag a project onto a folder, or use **Move to …** in its menu. A project is in one folder at a time.
- Click a folder's name to open or close it. Rename or delete it from its menu; deleting a folder keeps its projects.
- **Recent projects** shows the projects you opened last in one row. Everything not in a folder is listed below it.

**Jump points**
- The jump points list is easier to use: each jump point is a box in your accent color that opens it, and the **X** to remove it sits at the end of its row.
- **Drag a jump point out of the list** into a tool: on a board it becomes a jump point on the board, in text (Writer, Wiki and every other text) it becomes a `[[link`, and on an entry's **Jump to** bar it becomes a link.

**Story Branch Writer**
- New nodes start bigger, with a line between the bold title and the story text.
- **Double-click** a node to type its title and story text right on it, without handles or outlines in the way. **Enter** in the title jumps to the story text; **Shift+Tab** goes back; **Esc** or clicking away finishes.

**Smoother boards on Linux**
- Moving things on Brainstorm, Moodboard, Story, Timeline, Map and UI Flow boards no longer lags behind the mouse in the Linux app and Flatpak. The board now redraws once per screen refresh instead of for every tiny mouse move.

**Import from Git without an address**
- **Import from Git** on the start screen connects to GitHub by itself when Git on your computer is already logged in, and lists your projects there.
- **Import all** downloads every project from GitHub that is not on this computer yet. Projects you already have are marked.

**Level Layout**
- **Select and move** only picks up the tiles of the current layer, so moving a fence no longer drags the ground with it. Delete on a selection also only empties the current layer.

**License**
- Still free and open source under the GPL-3.0. One extra term: if you share a changed version, it has to visibly credit the original, Eve.Lopment Games Designer by Evelopment Games, with a link. Donations on Ko-fi are welcome, never required.
