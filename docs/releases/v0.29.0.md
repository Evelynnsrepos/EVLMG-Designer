## What's new in 0.29.0: the Pixel Art rework

Pixel Art got a big rework: sharp pixels at every zoom, live shapes you can edit later, Draw's colours and layers,
better tilesets and lots of new basics. Old pixel art opens as before. All new parts of the file are optional, so
older projects and the mobile app keep working.

**Sharp and smooth**
- Pixels stay **sharp** at every zoom (they were blurry on Linux), and zooming no longer slows down.
- **Ctrl + wheel** zooms in and out where the pointer is, also beside the picture. The wheel alone moves the picture
  and Shift + wheel moves it sideways. **Zoom to fit** centres it, and the **Hand** (H, Space or the middle button)
  drags it.
- Undo, redo, the **undo history** list and the reference button sit at the start of the toolbar.

**Live shapes you can edit later**
- **Line, Curve, Rectangle and Ellipse** make their own small layer above the one you drew on. Click that layer later
  and drag its points: a curve keeps both ends and the middle of its bend, and a rectangle or ellipse stays one when
  you drag a corner. **Turn into pixels** makes it normal pixels.
- Hold **Ctrl** (or Shift) while drawing or dragging a rectangle or ellipse for a **perfect square or circle**. This also
  works for the rectangle and ellipse selections. Taking away from a selection is now **Alt**.
- **Shape layers** keep editable shapes. Use the **Shape tool** (P) to click points, the bucket to fill a shape, and a
  rough pencil stroke becomes a clean shape. Shapes change their outline, fill and width any time.
- **In-betweens**: draw shapes on two key frames and fill the frames between them, with easing.

**Colours and layers like in Draw**
- Draw's colour panel (disc, classic, harmony, value, palettes) replaces the system colour box everywhere.
- **Bigger palette**. Every ready-made palette shows its colours before you open it.
- **Switching palettes** after you've drawn asks whether to add your colours to the new palette or swap them.
  Swapping lets you connect each old colour to a new one with cables, with a before-and-after preview.
- **Layers look like Draw's**, with thumbnails and all of Draw's **blend modes**. Layers can be put in **groups**
  that fold, hide, lock and move together. Opacity can also change per frame, for fades.
- **Palette and Layers pop out** to float over the canvas (the colour panel already could).
- **Reference images** float over the canvas like in Draw. Fill and the magic wand can look at this layer, all layers
  or the reference layer.

**Tilesets**
- The tile you work on is outlined. **Ctrl + arrow keys** go to the next tile and zoom in on it (on sprites: the next frame).
- The chain button on a layer shows it on **all tiles**.
- **Preview** plays the tiles as an animation, one frame per tile.

**New basics**
- Canvas size, trim and crop for the whole sprite. Flip and turn the whole sprite.
- Selections: ellipse (J), polygon lasso (N), grow, shrink, border and invert. Selecting can be undone.
- Drag the handles of a selection to scale or rotate it.
- Fill and the magic wand have a **Tolerance**.
- **Adjust colours** (hue, saturation, lightness, brightness, contrast, invert) and **Effects** (drop shadow, remove
  stray pixels).
- Outline inside or around the drawing, with or without corners. Palette sort and remove unused colours.
- Drag frames in the timeline to reorder them. Tags can **repeat** a number of times.
- Sprite sheets also export as a GameMaker strip.

**Draw**
- The Animation mode saves **sprite sheets** for Godot 4 (.tres), GameMaker and PNG + JSON, with a size and a shared trim.

**Undo for everything**
- Every change in Pixel Art can be undone and redone. A slider drag or moving a reference image is one undo step.

Known limits: this was tested in the browser, not yet on every system. Line and shape layers have no "merge into the
layer below" yet: use Turn into pixels on their own layer.
