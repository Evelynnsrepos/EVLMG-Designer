## What's new in 0.32.2: a new Story Branch Writer

The Story Branch Writer got a full overhaul: easier to start with, more useful for bigger stories, and it now fits the glass look. Old stories open unchanged.

**A fresh look**
- Scenes are rounded cards with a soft wash of their branch colour and a colour stripe on the left.
- The side panel, the bar under a scene and Play through use the glass look (Full, Light or Off in Settings) and your accent colour.

**Easier to write branches**
- Select a scene and use the bar under it: **+ Add next** puts a new scene to the right. Add a second one and it becomes a choice.
- **Ending** marks a scene as one of the story's endings. In the details you can also mark **the Start**.
- The empty canvas shows three short steps to get going.

**Choices and checks**
- Every arrow can have its own **choice text**, the words the player picks, in the scene's details.
- With nothing selected, the side panel shows your story: scenes, choice points, endings and words.
- **Story check** lists lines that stop without an ending and scenes no path reaches. Click one to go there.

**Play it, tidy it, take it along**
- **Play through** (P) reads your story like a player: click a choice or press its number, Backspace goes back, Esc stops.
- **Tidy up** lays the whole story out left to right, one column per step.
- **Export for Twine** saves a .twee file that Twine and other story engines open.
- The **Design Book** has a new Stories chapter with every scene and links between the choices.
- Hold **Q** for the Hotwheel: the story tools, + Add next, Ending, Play and more.

Everything you change can be undone with Ctrl+Z.
