## What's new in 0.25.1: exports that match

**Card Designer**
- **The export window remembers your exports.** Every export is saved with its settings, one for each kind (every print shop on its own). Under Cards, "Last export like this" shows when you exported, the card size, the total bleed and how it is made up, and the other settings. Anything that differs from your current settings is shown in orange, and "Use these settings again" puts them back.
- **Match deck: two decks that come out the same.** Have two versions of a set in different Card Designer decks? Pick the other deck under Match deck. The window tells you whether your settings will come out like that deck's last export, and "Use the same settings" copies them. If the two cards have different bleeds of their own, the added bleed is adjusted so the total bleed still matches. A list shows every export of the other deck, marked same, different or not exported here yet; click one to set it up, then press Export. Exports made before this update were not saved, so export each option once more.
- **Photo station (janky): even folding.** When the back sits next to the front, both now get their whole bleed, including in the middle. Crop marks show both cut lines with the fold line between them, so the pair folds evenly. Fold it backwards along the dashed mark, glue it, then cut through both layers.
