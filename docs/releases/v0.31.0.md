## What's new in 0.31.0: a new drawing engine, Material Lab, glass look

A big one: Draw now paints on the graphics card, Material Lab gets its screens, Texture Paint can fill parts and add wear,
Vector learns to combine shapes and draw with a pen, and the whole app can wear a glass look. Older projects open as before.

**Draw: painting on the graphics card**
- Brushes, blend modes, wet paint, smudge, adjustment layers and the Move tool now run on the graphics card. Big pictures
  with many layers stay smooth. If the graphics card can't be used, Draw falls back to the old engine by itself.
- On Linux, every mouse and pen sample now reaches the app, and pen pressure is read at most every 10 ms, so quick strokes
  no longer "stick". Saving no longer freezes the drawing: Draw saves at good moments (adding or moving a layer, switching
  tools, leaving the window, closing) and shows a thin line under the top bar while it saves. Other tools show a small dot.
- Paint and move things past the edge of the canvas: nothing is cut off, and exports still use the canvas you picked.
- Undo history is kept when you close the app: the last 100 steps in Draw and the whole history in Pixel Art.
- Layer picker (`K`, or hold Ctrl with a brush): click a spot to jump to the layer that painted it.
- Brush Studio: ribbon brushes, cap images and "Keep the image's light and dark".
- The speed test in Settings says which engine is drawing.

**Material Lab (experimental) gets its screens**
- Start from a gallery of ready materials, change them with simple sliders or Surprise me, and see them big on a lit ball,
  tiled or as pixel art with five lights. The Graph view lets you move, connect and add nodes, all undoable.
  Save the maps as PNGs in a ZIP.

**Texture Paint (experimental)**
- Fill a whole part with one click (a loose piece, a flat side, a ring of faces, a UV island, a material…).
- Add shape maps baked from the model: shadows in corners, seams, edges and creases, thickness, sky, height.
- Add wear where it belongs (worn edges, dirt, dust, rain streaks, drips, grime) with How much and Break up sliders.

**Vector (experimental), part 2**
- Combine shapes: join, cut out, keep or remove the overlap, split, take apart, shape builder, grow and shrink, outline to shape.
- Pen, pencil and point editing, SVG import, number boxes, align and spread, groups, copy and paste, snapping and crisp pixels.
- More looks: gradients in any direction or round, dashed and sharp outlines, glow, copy and paste a look; fonts, bold,
  italic and alignment for text; arrow and line shapes; its own Hotwheel. Every action can be undone.

**Glass look**
- Settings > General > Glass look: Full, Light or Off. The Hotwheel turns into glass bubbles with icons and curved size
  and opacity sliders. The accent colour is used everywhere the app used to be blue.

**For other versions and the mobile app:** drawings can have a new optional `frame`, undo history lives in a new
`.history` folder, Vector has a new shape kind `vpath` and Material Lab adds the `material` document type
(see docs/FORMAT.md). Older versions open these projects but don't know the new parts.
