## What's new in 0.26.0: a light preview in Draw

**Light preview (Draw and Texture Paint)**
- **New lamp button in the top bar.** It opens a small floating 3D view to see where light and shadows fall before you paint them.
- **Objects:** balls, cubes, cylinders, cones and rings, or your own model (GLB, glTF, OBJ, FBX), up to 8 at once. Move them along the floor, change their size and turn them. Give them any colour, or your brush colour, and more or less shine.
- **A planar head and a posable figure.** The head button adds a drawing study head built from big flat planes. The figure button adds a mannequin with poses (stand, walk, run, sit, crouch, reach up, point), and every joint can be fine-tuned.
- **Up to 4 lamps.** Drag a lamp's sun around the objects and set its strength, colour and distance. Sun light is parallel, a bulb shines from one point.
- **Light setups:** sunny, cloudy, studio, rim light, from below and window, ready with one click. Bounce colours the light coming up from the ground, and contact shadows darken the spots where things touch.
- **Views:** soft clay shading, 3 tones (lit, half tone, shadow) or only the shadow shapes, plus a grey mode to check values.
- **Use it while drawing:** the pipette picks a colour from the preview as your brush colour, and the pin puts the current view next to your drawing as a reference picture. The lock keeps the view at your drawing's angle.
- Your setup is remembered on this device. It is not saved into the drawing, so project files stay the same as before.

**Experimental: Material Lab engine**
- The engine for Material Lab, a coming tool for making seamless game materials from nodes, is now inside the app. It uses the node library from Material Maker. There is no Material Lab screen yet, so you won't see it in the app. It is experimental and here so it can be tested before the tool arrives in a later version.
- Material Lab's node library is from Material Maker by Rodolphe Suescun and contributors, under the MIT License. Thank you!
