## What's new in 0.13.0

Still no generative AI: every idea and every stroke is yours.

**Draw** (fixes from user reports)
- Adjustment layers: hue, saturation and brightness, colour balance, curves or a gradient map that change everything below them, and can be changed or removed any time. Clip one to a layer to change only that layer.
- Layers can clip to a group, and clipped layers now follow their base: they fade with its opacity and hide with it.
- Pick several layers without a keyboard (the tick-list button in the Layers header).
- The flip buttons mirror the whole picture (Shift-click: only the layer), and their icons now match what they do.
- While transforming, drag just outside any corner to turn. Snapping to the middle line is off by default, so things can be placed near it.
- The selection tool keeps the shape you picked (lasso, ellipse, wand) instead of going back to the rectangle.
- The colour disc is really round now: every spot in it is a colour, and dragging past its edge keeps working.
- Sliders follow a pen properly.

**Levels and maps**
- Level Layout: add tileset images (drawings from Draw work too), paint, fill and erase with picture tiles, and export the level as a Godot 4 TileMap scene with its TileSet.
- Map Creator and the other boards: shortcuts keep working after clicking a side panel or a toolbar button.

**Everywhere**
- Names: about 50 real-world name styles (German, Polish, Canadian, Japanese and many more) with a search field, as real names or new ones that sound like them.
- Pitch Deck: drag slides to reorder them.
- Projects can have a picture on the start screen (project menu, Add a picture).
- New project presets: Just write a story, Drawing and art, Worldbuilding, Level design, Tabletop, and Plan and pitch.
- A plain click in the sidebar always shows just that tool (no more question), Shift-click opens it next to the others, and right-click renames a document.
- New projects show a reminder that Esc rearranges and closes tools.
