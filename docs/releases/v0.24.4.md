## What's new in 0.24.4: card exports with pictures work again

**Card Designer**
- **Exports with pictures work.** Exporting a deck that has pictures on its cards (Tabletop Simulator, PNG, PDF and print sheets) stopped with "The operation is insecure". The pictures now load in a way the export is allowed to save, so every export works. The card list and layer thumbnails of cards with pictures work again too, since they load pictures the same way.

**Also in this version**
- Everything from 0.24.3: Duplicate a document from its right-click menu in the sidebar.
