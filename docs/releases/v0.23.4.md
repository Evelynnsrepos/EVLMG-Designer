## What's new in 0.23.4: Level Layout brushes and Scatter

**See before you click**
- A see-through **preview** follows the mouse and shows exactly what a click puts down: the stamp's tiles, the plan tile's colour, or the outline of what Erase, Blocked and Scatter cover. Faint cells are blocked and are left alone.
- **Snap** next to the brush: **Cell** puts the stamp on the cell under the mouse, **Stamp grid** only lets it land on a grid as big as itself, so pieces always line up, even across separate strokes.

**Fences and paths in one drag**
- Dragging with a stamp now puts down **whole copies side by side**, one each time the mouse has moved a full stamp further, instead of smearing it.
- Hold **Shift** while dragging to keep any brush on a straight line.

**Scatter (R)**
- Spread details at random, like rocks in a field or different stones in a path.
- Make a **scatter set** with + on the right, then click tiles in the tileset panel to add them. Drag over several tiles to add them as **one group** (a 2 × 2 boulder) that always goes down whole. Each detail has its own **how often** slider; the scissors split a group back into single tiles.
- **Amount**, **Spacing**, **Clumping** (even to patches) and **Only on** (tiles on a layer below, or the tile you start on: start on the path to put stones only on the path).
- Paint with the brush, switch to **Fill area** to scatter over a whole connected area, or scatter **In selection**. **Re-roll** tries a new random layout. Right-click takes the set's details out again.
- Scatter sets are saved with the tilesets, so every level in the project can use them. Scatter only paints on the picked layer.
