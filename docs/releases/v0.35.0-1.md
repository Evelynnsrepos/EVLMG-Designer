## Unstable pre-release 0.35.0-1

> **This is an unstable test build, not a normal release.** It has new tools and big changes that are not fully tested yet. Back up your projects before you open them with it. The newest stable version is still **0.34.0**. Installed apps do not update to this build on their own, and the Flatpak from this page does not update itself.

**New tools**
- **Status Effects & Reactions:** Burn, Freeze, Stun and your own effects with durations, stacks, ticks and stat changes, and a grid of what happens when two elements meet. Combat Simulator and Abilities use them.
- **Relationships:** friendship, romance and loyalty ranks per character, with gifts, unlocks and how long each rank takes.
- **Live-Service Planner:** versions, seasons, events, banners, a battle pass and the store after launch.
- **Behaviour Designer:** how enemies and NPCs decide what to do, with states, plain-word conditions, a step-through test and daily routines.
- **Economy Simulator** gets a Meters tab, **Gacha & Loot** an Offers mode for pick-one shops and drafts, and **Damage Calculator** time to kill over distance and a score stack.

**Brushes**
- About 50 new brushes: ropes, chains, ribbons, plants, hair and comic outlines.
- Brush Studio has a new Easy page, and brushes can have an outline.

**Everything else**
- The Add tool menu is grouped (Plan, Write, World, Art, Levels, Numbers, More). Story Writer, Design Language and Resource Calculator are hidden there, and their documents still open.
- Draw together: one person draws at a time, the others see who is drawing and can ask to take over.
- Settings: interface size, strong contrast, and German (partly translated).
- Safer file access and a safer app window. If a project on another drive doesn't open, pick its folder once with Open.
- Plugins say what they use before they load.
- Update check on the start screen (for the next stable versions).
- Storyboard video has sound, plus subtitle and sound-only export. Level Layout exports Tiled maps. Writer can print to PDF. Pixel Art saves layered pixel art files. Material Lab saves a Godot .tres. Big Card Designer decks show every card picture.
