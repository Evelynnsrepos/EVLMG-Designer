## What's new in 0.18.0

Still no generative AI: every idea and every stroke is yours.

**The Hotwheel**
- Hold `Q` anywhere in a project and a round menu opens under your pointer or pen. Point at a slice and let go, or tap `Q` and click. Esc or the centre cancels.
- The inner ring is the same everywhere: close the tool, its size, add a jump point, Layout Mode, Undo | Redo and search. The outer ring holds up to ten actions of the tool you are in, like Pencil, Fill and Pencil size in Pixel Art, or Sticky note, Pen and Jump point on boards.
- You don't have to aim: the direction counts, a short move picks the inner ring and a longer one the outer ring. Some slices have two halves, like Smaller | Bigger. Shift-click keeps the wheel open.
- It springs open with a little turn and shrinks away when it closes, with round, spaced slices and a readable name in the middle.
- For pens and tablets: open it with the pen's side button, by holding the right mouse button, or with the middle mouse button (Settings, Hotwheel). Small, medium or large, and it grows with the tool's own size.
- Change the inner ring in Settings and each tool's outer ring with the keyboard button in its header.
- Draw's QuickMenu is now Draw's ring on the Hotwheel, with ten slots; your QuickMenu profiles stay (Pen and keys, Hotwheel). Pixel Art's lasso moved from `Q` to `K`.
- Plugins can put their own actions on the wheel. See docs/PLUGINS.md; the Dice Roller example plugin shows how.

**Every tool's header**
- A keyboard button lists the tool's shortcuts and the ones that work everywhere, explains how to link things with text, jump points and Shift-click, and holds the tool's Hotwheel ring.
- A size button makes the whole tool 75 to 200 % bigger, for pens and graphics tablets. It is remembered per tool on your computer.

**Jump points**
- Add a jump point did nothing: the list sat under its own click-away layer. It works now, and the list is bigger.
- In any text, `[[` also links to tools and documents: a jump point right in your writing. Click it to go there.

**The user guide (F1)**
- New Start here section: the basics of Esc and Shift-click, the shortcuts cheat sheet, a crash course for the whole program (sidebar organising, linking with text, jump points and more) and the Hotwheel.
- Tools in detail has a search bar, and every tool page ends with its own shortcuts.

**Pictures**
- A picture with an empty transparent border (a small drawing on a big Draw canvas) is cut to what is drawn when you send it from Draw or put it on a Moodboard, Design Language or Brainstorm board. The original file stays as it is.
- The colour wheel window on boards no longer closes when you click it.
