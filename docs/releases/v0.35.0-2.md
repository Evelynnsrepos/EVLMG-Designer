## Unstable pre-release 0.35.0-2

> **This is an unstable test build, not a normal release.** It has new tools and big changes that are not fully tested yet. Back up your projects before you open them with it. The newest stable version is still **0.34.0**. Installed apps do not update to this build on their own, and the Flatpak from this page does not update itself.

Everything from 0.35.0-1, plus these new Draw features (all experimental):

**ASCII art**
- ASCII layers turn your painting into letters live, and an ASCII adjustment layer does the same for the layers below it.
- Quick looks: Classic, Newspaper, Terminal, Amber, Blocks, Line art, Lines only, Braille and Code, plus Your text (your own words written into the shape).
- Sign brushes paint the letters directly: Line, Shade, Subtract, Sign, Scatter and Auto. Invert and Scatter settings.
- Letter brush on the Brush Studio Easy page.
- Export as text, HTML, ANSI (for terminals) or an image. Pixel Art can export a frame as ASCII art.

**Outlines**
- Outline layer: a live outline around everything on a layer (outside, center or inside, one or two colours). It follows when you keep painting.
- Outliner tool: draws an outline around a drawing or a selection onto a new layer.

**Turn and bend drawings**
- Rotate object (R): turns a drawing as a 3D shape with thickness, round or straight sides and light.
- Mold object (W): bend a drawing with pins. Keep shape makes arms and legs bend instead of stretching.
- Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y step through changes while you move, turn or mold.
- Values to opacity (Adjustments menu): makes the bright or the dark parts see-through.

**Animated brushes**
- Brushes can wiggle in a loop. Painting with them makes an Animated layer.
- Export the animation as GIF, APNG, WebM or a sprite sheet.

**Smoother drawing**
- Each pen move shows one screen frame sooner, and just moving the mouse over the canvas no longer slows the graphics card. Painting tools keep the normal crosshair.

**Files:** projects saved with this build stay readable in 0.34.0 and in the mobile app. There, Outline, ASCII and Animated layers show only the painting under the effect (Animated layers: the first frame).
