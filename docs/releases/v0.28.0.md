## What's new in 0.28.0: studio tools, a new Storyboard, Custom Lists and better shape snapping

This is a big one: everything built since 0.27.0 comes together here. Old projects open as before; all new parts of
the project file are optional, so the mobile app and older projects keep working.

**Storyboard (much bigger)**
- **Scenes, cuts and panels** with automatic numbers, and timing in seconds + frames (24, 25, 30 or 60 fps) with totals and timecode.
- Every panel has shot, camera, transition, notes, characters and a status. **Board view** shows the pictures, **Sheet view** puts the text next to every picture.
- **Writer links:** link a panel to a Writer document or heading, or make panels **From Writer…** (one per heading or per paragraph).
- **Draw this panel** opens a drawing in the board's size with the old picture as the bottom layer and the previous panel as a reference. The board updates while you draw.
- **Animatic** with a soundtrack, sound clips per panel and safe-area lines.
- **Export:** PDF (storyboard sheet with 5 panels per page, overview, one panel per page for presenting), animatic as WebM or GIF with subtitles, shot list CSV, script as Markdown, PNG ZIP, or straight into the Pitch Deck as slides.

**Custom List (new tool)**
- Make your own kind of entry (vehicles, perks, biomes…) with your own fields: text, long text, numbers with a unit, yes/no, choice lists, links to entries and pictures.
- Templates for an empty list: Controls & input, Accessibility checklist, Glossary and Risk register.
- Custom List entries can be `[[linked]]` everywhere links work, and Writer cards (`![[`) show their live values.

**Spreadsheet**
- `=STAT("Iron Sword", "Damage")` reads a value live from your lists and Custom Lists. Type over a STAT cell to change the entry itself.
- Right-click → **Insert live table of …** puts a whole list into the sheet and keeps it up to date.

**Localization**
- Translations stay with the right line, even when you rename things or move lines around.
- When the source text changes, its translation turns orange. Filter **Needs update** and tick a text when it is checked.
- **For translators…** saves a language as XLIFF or PO (Godot reads PO).

**Engine export**
- Every data tool is now exported (abilities, crafting, economy, skill tree, timeline, wave planner, Custom Lists and more), plus `flags.json` with named conditions.
- Level Layout markers become Marker2D nodes in Godot.
- Build servers can export without opening the app: `npm run export -- <project folder> godot|unity <output folder>`.

**Team work**
- Git sync merges changes from two computers by itself when they touch different lines (after a backup). It only asks you when the same lines changed.
- Reviews remember who approved and when, and show **changed since** with an **Approve again** button.
- New clock button in every panel header: see earlier versions of a document from the backups and restore one (undo works).
- Comment connect codes: a person can look at everything and only write review comments and statuses. This needs a server version that supports comment codes, which is not out yet.

**Simulators and production**
- Combat Sim uses abilities (with cooldowns) and resistances. Economy has player types (casual, regular, hardcore) and **Add quest gold**. Balance check also checks abilities and Custom List numbers.
- Task Board: estimates in hours, milestones with progress, **Waits for** blockers, name suggestions and **CSV for Jira/Linear**.
- Playtests: turn a finding into a task. Scope Planner: split a feature's days by discipline.
- Dialogue lines get a voice direction and voice file, with an **Actor script** CSV export. Pitch Deck saves a **PDF**. Writer offers templates (feature spec, level brief, one-page overview, postmortem).

**Tools work together better**
- **Asset Pool:** attach any file (sounds play right there, models and source files are kept), who / due / priority, **Make a task** and a main picture. Asset Pool entries show up in Ctrl+K and in `[[ ]]` links.
- **Send to:** Vector, the Card Designer and Texture Paint can send pictures; the Card Designer can receive them (onto the active layer). Vector also exports all icons for Godot. Plugins can use Send to too.
- **One project palette:** "This project (shared)" shows in every colour panel and travels with the project.
- Draw's light preview can use 3D models that are already in the project, and they come back next time.
- The Design Book gets chapters for assets, card decks and icons. The Art template now includes Pixel Art, Texture Paint and the Card Designer.

**Drawing: shape snap**
- Draw roughly and keep holding: circles (also when you go round several times), tilted ellipses and boxes that stay tilted, and smooth curves that become arcs. Keep holding a bit longer for a perfect shape.
- The Pixel Art pencil snaps shapes too, in whole pixels.
- Pen and keys → Smoothing has a **Holding still** slider and **Shaky hands** / **Steady hands** buttons. Motion filtering also steadies the Pixel Art pencil.
- Holding to snap now allows a bit more wobble than before, so a snap can happen a little more easily. Lower Holding still if that bothers you.

**Hotkey hints**
- Hotkey hint mode is now on for everyone: buttons show their key in the corner. Turn it off in Settings → General.

**Still experimental:** Vector, Texture Paint and Material Lab (no screen yet). New in this version and not yet tried on a real tablet or in every app window: shape snap, Shaky hands and the new Storyboard exports.
