## What's new in 0.24.3: duplicate documents

**Sidebar**
- **Duplicate.** Right-click a document under a tool (a card deck, a drawing, a board, a level…) and choose Duplicate. You get a full copy called "… (copy)" that opens right away. Change the copy as much as you like, the original stays as it was. Handy for turning your card template into a dummy deck to test with.

**Card Designer**
- **Exports with pictures work.** Exporting a deck that has pictures on its cards (Tabletop Simulator, PNG, PDF and print sheets) stopped with "The operation is insecure". The pictures now load in a way the export is allowed to save, so every export works. The card list and layer thumbnails of cards with pictures work again too.

**Also in this version**
- Everything from 0.24.2: the Bring back button for lost layers in Draw, and floating panels that stay on screen.
