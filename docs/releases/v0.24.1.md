## What's new in 0.24.1: pen tablets on Linux and Tabletop Simulator export

**Draw**
- **Real pen pressure and tilt on Linux.** The web view on Linux passed drawing tablets through as a mouse, so pressure and tilt were lost. The app now reads them straight from the pen driver, in the installed app and in the Flatpak.
- **Clean stroke ends.** When the pen lifts, some tablets briefly report zero pressure; those points are ignored now, so strokes no longer end in a hair-thin tail. With real pen pressure, brushes skip their artificial end taper: the line you see while drawing is the line you keep.
- **Magic wand on the active layer.** The wand looks only at the active layer (or the reference layer, if you set one), not at the whole picture.
- **More undo.** Selections, every layer change, the background colour and guides are now undo and redo steps.
- **Rounded corners** for rectangle selections.
- **Brush outline** stays visible on light parts of the picture.
- **Hotwheel:** Smaller and Bigger on the inner ring change the brush size.

**Card Designer**
- **Export for Tabletop Simulator (ZIP).** Card sheets and the back picture, ready for Objects > Components > Cards > Custom Deck. The grid size and card count are in the file name. Decks with more than 69 cards are split into several sheets, and different backs per card come as their own backs sheet.

**Also in this version**
- Everything from 0.24.0: faster drawing on Linux and Drawing speed in Settings.
