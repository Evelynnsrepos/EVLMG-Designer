## What's new in 0.23.6: pictures, snapping and fields that fill themselves

**Card Designer**
- **Layers are on top** in the right panel; Style, Card size and Export are below them.
- **The image button asks for a picture** and imports it at its own shape, no more warping.
- **Drag and drop picture files** straight onto the card.
- **Right-click a picture** to replace it, or fit it to the bleed, the cut line or the safe line.
- **Snapping**: the magnet button snaps items to the card edges, the safe line and the centre while you move or resize them.
- **+ Text, + Number and + Picture in Fields also add the box to the card**, ready to fill.
- **Typing in a text box shows the real font, colour, bold, italic and alignment.**
- **Renaming a field renames {Name} in every text box** once you finish typing (Enter or click away).
- Picture fields are named "Picture field" in the layers list.

**Start screen**
- The version number now shows next to the app name, handy when your window has no title bar.
