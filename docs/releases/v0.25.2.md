## What's new in 0.25.2: arranging tools without a keyboard

**Arranging tools**
- **New Arrange button next to Projects.** The top of the sidebar is now split in two: Projects on the left, Arrange on the right. Arrange does exactly what Esc does: it turns Layout Mode on so you can move, resize and close tools. Tap it again when you are done. It is lit while Layout Mode is on. Handy on touch screens, tablets and pen displays.
- **Drag tools out of the sidebar with a finger or pen.** Slide a tool or document sideways out of the sidebar and let go on the side of an open tool to open it next to it, or on the empty workspace to open it there. With a mouse, dragging works as before.
- On a touch screen without a keyboard, the "Esc rearranges tools" reminder no longer stays up forever.

**Tour**
- The editor tour now shows the Arrange button and dragging with a finger or pen.
- The start-screen and editor tours teach the basics you need, so skipping them now asks you to confirm: type "I understand" to skip. You can replay any tour from Help.
