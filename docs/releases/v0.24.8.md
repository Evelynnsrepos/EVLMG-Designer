## What's new in 0.24.8: real bleed and a PDF layout choice

**Card Designer**
- **Bleed in mm or pixels.** Next to the bleed value there's now a unit switch: type it in mm, or in px if your art program thinks in pixels (pixels count at 300 DPI, so 36 px is about 3 mm). Works on the new deck screen and under Card size. Older decks keep their bleed.
- **The bleed is really added.** Every print export is now the card plus the bleed on every side: a 63 × 88 mm card with 3 mm bleed comes out 69 × 94 mm (at 300 DPI: 814 × 1109 px instead of 744 × 1039). Where your art stops at the cut line, the card's edge is stretched out into the bleed, so a slightly off cut never shows a white edge.
- **Bigger bleed, bigger background.** Making the bleed bigger grows the background (and any shape that reached the old bleed line) out to the new line.
- **PDF asks for the layout.** One card per page: every page is one card with its bleed, the file print shops ask for. Many cards per page: the print sheets with crop marks for printing at home, as before. Foil masks ask the same.
- **Card images with bleed.** Card images + JSON now saves every card with its bleed (deck.json says how much). Deck image and the Tabletop Simulator exports stay without bleed, so the cards look right on the table.
