## What's new in 0.14.0

Still no generative AI: every idea and every stroke is yours.

**Texture Paint (experimental)**
- A new tool to paint 3D models: the model on the left, its texture on the right, both updating as you paint. Paint straight on the model or on the texture, with every Draw brush, layer and adjustment.
- Import GLB (best), OBJ or FBX. A model that brings its own texture starts with it as a layer.
- UV layout adds the model's UV layout as a locked guide layer that stays out of exports.
- Export the texture like any drawing, or GLB to save the model with its painted texture for Godot, Unity or Unreal.
- It is experimental: a warning says so each time it opens.

**Level Layout**
- Mark where nothing can walk: the Blocked brush paints cells red, and tileset tiles can block movement by themselves (tick This tile blocks movement for fences, walls and houses).
- The Godot scene export gives blocking tiles collision and adds hand-blocked cells as a static body, so characters stop at fences and houses.
- Fast drags no longer leave gaps.

**Everywhere**
- Every slider in the app has the new look.
- Each tool has a small × in its header to close it, next to Review.
- Moodboard (and Design Language): an eraser (E) that removes drawn lines you drag over.
