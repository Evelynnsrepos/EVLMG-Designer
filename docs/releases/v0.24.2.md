## What's new in 0.24.2: bring back lost layers

**Draw**
- **Bring back lost panels.** A new button in the top bar (the target icon, next to Layers) opens a small menu: put the layers back floating at the top right, or back in the side panel. Use it whenever the layers are closed or out of sight.
- **Floating panels stay on screen.** The floating Layers and Text panels now always stay inside the window, also after the window gets smaller or the tool runs at a bigger size. Before, dragging one to the right edge of a zoomed tool could snap it out of sight, and a panel saved on a bigger window could open off-screen.

**Level Layout**
- The popped-out tilesets panel can no longer be dragged out of the window.

**Also in this version**
- Everything from 0.24.1: pen pressure and tilt on Linux, more undo in Draw and Tabletop Simulator export.
